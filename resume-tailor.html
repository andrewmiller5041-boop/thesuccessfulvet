Before you upload

We only work with what's actually in your resume — no invented numbers, employers, or tools. Anywhere your resume is missing a real number, we'll flag it so you can add your own instead of a made-up one. If the same job shows up at several duty stations, we'll combine those into one entry — real promotions stay separate. Old scanned resumes work too — we'll read them automatically if a direct text extraction doesn't work.
